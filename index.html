About

Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science student in the FIU Honors College, working toward a B.S. with a minor in Mathematical Sciences — and figuring out how to turn a genuine curiosity about security into a real career.

My interests sit at the intersection of vulnerability management, incident response, cloud security, and detection engineering. What draws me to this field is the investigative side — tracing how something broke, understanding why, and communicating it clearly to the people who need to know.

I learn best by doing: I set up home labs, dig into tools hands-on, and look for ways to apply classroom concepts to problems I encounter at work. Earning CompTIA Security+ in June 2026 was a milestone, but the tools and workflows I've picked up during my internship have taught me far more about what security work actually looks like day to day.
